What did Wall Street do after the midterms? It surged. Stocks took off. The Dow jumped 3 percent, adding more than fifteen hundred points. The S and P 500 and the Nasdaq climbed too. The key driver: Democrats won control of Congress. Uncertainty faded, the VIX fear gauge dropped, and oil prices tumbled. Microsoft led big tech, jumping more than six percent, and Amazon wasn't far behind. Apple bounced back too, climbing more than five percent and erasing yesterday's slide. Alphabet added about three percent, and Meta edged higher too. But not everything rallied. Nvidia slipped about three percent, and Tesla slid by a similar amount. Stay with us for the next open.
